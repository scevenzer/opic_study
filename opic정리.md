각 항목은 기존 프로그램 구조(문제 / 번역 / 스크립트)에 맞춰 정리했습니다.

## 1. 영화관 묘사

**문제**: Which theater do you go to most often? Where is it located? What does it look like inside and outside? What kinds of movies are usually shown there?

**최종 원고**
Yeah, I like all kinds of movies. I usually go to the movies with my wife. The theater we go to most often is called CGV. It's about a five-minute walk from our place. It's inside a big shopping mall called Beta Bolt Mall, so there's not much to say about the outside. Inside, the first thing you see is the ticket counter, and they have screens showing which movies are selling well. They show pretty much everything there, from Hollywood movies to Korean films and animated movies. My wife and I really enjoy going there, and she especially loves the popcorn. It's a nice place for us to relax and spend time together.

**핵심 표현**
- there's not much to say about the outside — 외관 특별할 거 없다는 자연스러운 표현
- screens showing which movies are selling well — 인기 영화 안내 스크린 디테일
- from A to B and C — 여러 종류 나열할 때
- a nice place to relax and spend time together — 마무리용 개인 감상 표현

## 3. 집과 방 소개 (가장 좋아하는 방)

**문제**: 어디 사는지 알려줘. 집이랑 방들을 설명해줘. 방들이 어떻게 생겼는지, 가장 좋아하는 방은 어디고 자세히 설명해줘.

**최종 원고 (IH 버전)**
Well, I recently moved into a new house, and it's actually a downtown apartment on the 2nd floor. Honestly, I'm really glad about that, because I used to live on the 22nd floor, and it was terrible whenever the elevator was out of service. I remember having to climb all those stairs — it was exhausting. Anyway, my favorite room in my new house is the computer room. It has a computer, a couple of game machines like a Nintendo Switch and a PlayStation, and a few board games as well. On weekends, I usually spend time there playing games with my son, and honestly, that time means a lot to me. It's one of my favorite parts of the week.

**핵심 표현**
- it was terrible whenever ~ — ~할 때마다 힘들었다
- a couple of / a few — 몇 개의 (게임기, 보드게임 나열할 때)
- that time means a lot to me — 그 시간이 나에게 의미 있다 (I feel special 오류 대체 표현)

## 5. 집 문제 - 층간소음 해결

**문제**: 집에서 있었던 문제와 해결 과정을 설명해줘 (home problem and resolution)

**최종 원고**
Well, one of the most memorable problems we had at home was noise from upstairs.
After we moved in, we sometimes heard kids running around, and honestly, it started to bother us a little.
At first, we tried not to make a big deal out of it, but over time, it became uncomfortable.
So I decided to talk to my upstairs neighbor and tried to understand their situation too.
I also told the apartment office, and they made an announcement about the noise.
It wasn't a perfect solution, but things improved.
I learned that talking to neighbors is really important.

**핵심 표현**
- make a big deal out of it — 크게 문제 삼다 (항상 단수 형태로 사용)
- things improved — 상황이 나아졌다 (간단 버전)
- Through that experience, I realized ~ — 경험을 통한 깨달음으로 마무리 (IH~AL 마무리 패턴)

## 6. 주거 변화 (어릴 때 vs 지금)

**문제**: 3번 문제 - 어릴 때부터 지금까지 사는 곳이 어떻게 변화했는지 설명

**최종 원고**
My family used to live in a small apartment when I was a child. We just had two bedrooms, one kitchen, and one bathroom. Also, we did not have many pieces of furniture. I did not have a bed in my room, so I used to sleep on the floor. Then, I would wake up with a stiff back the next morning.

Now, everything is different. I live in a bigger and fancier apartment. It has three bedrooms, a kitchen, a living room, and a bathroom. And guess what? We have beds now! I can get a more comfortable sleep on the bed than before. Plus, we even have built-in furniture like walk-in closets and drawers. So, my apartment looks neater and more spacious now.

**핵심 표현**
- wake up with a stiff back — 뻐근한 등으로 잠에서 깨다 (구어체)
- guess what? — 그거 알아? (놀라움/전환 표현)
- more spacious — (공간이) 더 넓은
- consists of → has — 격식체 대신 동사 중심 구어 표현

## 7. 한국의 재활용 시스템

**문제**: Tell me about recycling in your country. How do people recycle? What are some things that are recycled?

**최종 원고 (기본 답변)**
That's an interesting question. Actually, South Korea is quite strict about recycling. We have to separate our trash into categories like paper, glass, cans, and plastic. Besides that, food waste needs to be separated too, and we even have to buy special biodegradable bags for it. In my neighborhood, we throw out recycling on specific days, so if you miss the day, you have to keep it at home until the next collection. As far as I know, Korea's recycling system is considered one of the best in the world, and I think it's because the rules are so detailed and strictly enforced.

**후속 답변용 (콤보 질문 대비 - 지역별 차이)**
Also, recycling days depend on where you live. In the past, you could throw out recycling anytime you wanted, but now, in my area, it's only allowed on Sundays.

**핵심 표현**
- As far as I know (❌ As I know) — 내가 아는 한 (콩글리시 주의)
- separate A into B, C, and D — ~를 ~로 나누다
- depend on ~ — ~에 따라 다르다

> ⚠️ 전략 메모: 콤보 문제 대비, 첫 답변은 사실 위주로 두고 개인 경험/디테일은 후속 질문에 대비해 아껴두기

## 8. 국내여행 - 좋아하는 여행지 (만리포 해변)

**문제**: You indicated that you take vacations domestically. Tell me some places you like to travel to and why.

**최종 원고**
The place I often travel to domestically is Manripo Beach, on the west coast of Korea. It's big enough to hold about ten thousand people. There are a few reasons I like going there. First, it's pretty close to my place. It takes about two hours by car, so I don't need to spend much money on transportation. Also, it has better facilities than other beaches. For example, there are nice hotels, good restaurants, famous attractions, and beautiful cafes. Overall, it's a great place to visit.

**핵심 표현**
- big enough to hold about X people — 규모를 사람 수로 설명하는 표현
- pretty close to my place — 집에서 가깝다 (자연스러운 구어체)
- spend much money on transportation — 교통비를 많이 쓰다
- better facilities than other beaches — 비교 대상을 구체적으로 지정(다른 "장소"가 아니라 다른 "해변")해야 자연스러움

> ✅ 검토 결과: 질문(좋아하는 국내 여행지 + 이유)에 정확히 대응하는 구조 — 장소 소개 → 이유 두 가지(거리/시설) → 구체 예시 → 마무리. 그대로 사용하기 적합해서 data.js(국내여행 1번, id 98) 원고도 이 버전으로 업데이트했습니다.

## 9. 국내여행 - 여행 준비 과정

**문제**: Can you tell me all the things you do in order to prepare for a trip?

**최종 원고**
When I get ready for a trip, I usually follow a certain routine. First, I decide on the destination and the plan. After that, I search online for information about the place — nice hotels, good restaurants, famous attractions, and transportation. Then I book a hotel and transportation, usually online, because it's fast and convenient. Before I leave, I pack my things — clothes, underwear, a light jacket, and some snacks. And that's basically my routine before a trip.

**핵심 표현**

## 10. 국내여행 - 어릴 때 여행 (속초 해변)

**문제**: Tell me about some of the trips you took in your youth. Where did you go, who with, and what did you do?

When I was in middle school, I had a memorable experience with a trip. At the time, my family wanted to do something fun, so we decided to go to the beach. We went to Sokcho Beach, on the east coast of Korea. It was really crowded, especially with couples, so I was a bit annoyed. As soon as we got there, we found a spot to rest and unpacked. After that, we grabbed some food and talked about our day. Well, it was such a great time!

## 11. 롤플레이(11-12-13번) 필수 표현/패턴 정리

data.js에 있는 롤플레이 12세트(MP3 구매/가구 구매/휴대폰 구매/옷 구매/저녁 초대/공원 약속/공연 티켓/여행 예약/병원 예약/식당/지리·친구방문/날씨와 옷차림)를 전부 훑어서, 공통으로 반복되는 뼈대만 뽑았습니다. 실전에서는 이 틀에 그날의 상황(물건/장소/이유)만 바꿔 끼우면 됩니다.

### 11번 — 질문하기 (전화로 정보 요청, 질문 3~4개)
1. **전화+인사**: `Hello, is this [상대]? This is [내 이름]. How are you?` / 가게 상대일 땐 `Hi there, is this the [가게]?`
2. **용건**: `Well, I'd like to / I want to ~ because ~. I have some questions about it.`
3. **질문① 추천**: `What kinds of ~ are popular these days? Can you give me any recommendations?`
4. **질문② 특징/기능**: `What [features/functions] do they have? I [개인적인 필요] — do you think they have that?`
5. **질문③ 가격**: `What's the price(range)? I have a budget of $[X] — can I get a discount?`
6. **마무리**: `Awesome, that's the answer I wanted. Thank you for the information. Bye.`

### 12번 — 문제 해결하기 (상황 설명 + 대안 2~3개 제시)
1. **전화+용건**: `Hello, is this [상대]? I'm calling about ~.`
2. **사과+상황 설명**: `I'm sorry, but ~ — [이유/증상].`
3. **대안① 일정 조정**: `So, I wonder if we could reschedule ~. How about [다른 날짜/시간]?`
4. **대안②**: `Or, can we/I just ~?`
5. **마무리**: `Please call me back when you get this message. / Thank you for understanding. Bye.`

### 13번 — 관련 경험 말하기
1. **도입**: `Let me tell you about my experience from about two years ago.`
2. **기대**: `At first, everything seemed fine. I was excited to ~.`
3. **전환(문제 발생)**: `However, ~.`
4. **심화**: `To make matters worse, ~.`
5. **시도**: `So I tried to ~, but it wasn't easy.`
6. **결과**: `As a result, I had to ~.`
7. **마무리**: `Nevertheless, ~. / It was such a disappointing experience.`

### 참고 — 아직 13번이 없는 세트
식당(id52) / 지리·친구방문(id53) / 날씨와 옷차림(id54)은 11-12번만 있고 13번(관련경험) 원고가 아직 없음. 나중에 채울 때 위 13번 패턴 그대로 활용하면 됨.
